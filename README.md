# Eating Animation — Watch Food Shrink with Every Single Bite

Eating Animation 9minecraft Mod adds visible bite animations to items whenever you eat or drink in the game. Food in your hand shrinks piece by piece instead of staying completely still while chewing. Drinkable items like potions and milk buckets tilt and drain as your character gulps them down. It works across every default meal and keeps your survival gameplay simple without adding extra clutter.

Food items show visible bites and shrink step by step while you eat.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Fring**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
